## How to use this list

Items **2** and **1** are the highest-value to close because they gate correctness (FPS) and full vertex-format support (data types). Both are resolvable with a single stock `.pod` hex-dump plus one `readelf` of the shipped `.so`. Everything else is either cosmetic (naming) or only matters for byte-exact round-tripping / raw-struct interop, which our tooling does not currently require.
